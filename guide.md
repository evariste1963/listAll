# listAll - User Guide

listAll is your personal list manager with three list types: Shopping, Memos, and Todos.

## Shopping Lists

- **Create**: Tap **+** on the Shopping tab. Name your list and start adding shops (e.g., Walmart, Costco).
- **Add items**: Select a shop tab, type your item, and press **+** or Enter.
- **Toggle items**: Tap **O** to mark pending, **✓** to mark done.
- **Delete completed**: Tap the trash icon to remove all checked items.
- **Add shops**: Tap **+ Add** in the shop tabs row, or long-press to delete a shop.
- **Edit items**: Tap on any item text to rename it.

## Memos

- **Create**: Tap **+** on the Memos tab, enter a title, and confirm.
- **Add lines**: Type and press Enter to add memo lines.
- **Toggle**: Tap **O** / **✓** to mark lines complete.
- **Delete**: Long-press a memo card to delete it and all its lines.

## Todos

- **Create**: Tap **+** on the Todos tab, enter a title, and confirm.
- **Add tasks**: Type your task and press **+** or Enter.
- **Due dates**: Tap the date button to set an optional due date.
- **Priority**: Tap the flag icon to cycle through **Low**, **Medium**, **High**.
- **Toggle/Delete/Edit**: Same as Memos — tap checkbox, long-press to delete, tap text to edit.

## Settings

Access via the **Prefs** tab:

- **Theme**: Switch between **Dark**, **Mid** (green), and **Light**.
- **Default shops**: Manage shops that auto-populate when creating new shopping lists.

## Tips

- Swipe or tap Back to navigate between screens.
- Long-press cards and items to reveal delete options.
- Lists persist locally — data is saved automatically.
